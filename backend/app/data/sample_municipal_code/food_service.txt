[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 8.14.100]
Food Establishment Permit. No person shall operate a restaurant, cafe, food
truck, or other food service establishment without a valid Food Establishment
Permit issued by the County Health Department. Application requires a plan
review of the kitchen layout, proof of a certified Food Protection Manager on
staff, and passage of a pre-opening health inspection. Fee: $310 annually for
establishments under 3,000 sq ft; $560 annually above that threshold.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 8.14.115]
Mobile Food Facility (Food Truck) Permit. Food trucks additionally require a
Mobile Food Facility Permit ($225/year) and must pass a fire-suppression
system inspection if equipped with a griddle, fryer, or open flame. Trucks
must obtain a commissary agreement letter from an approved licensed kitchen
for overnight storage and servicing.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 8.14.140]
Alcohol Service. Establishments serving beer, wine, or spirits for on-site
consumption must separately obtain a Type 41/47 equivalent license from the
State Alcoholic Beverage Control division AND a Local Alcohol Sales
Endorsement from the City Clerk. Processing time for the local endorsement
alone is 30-45 days and requires a public notice posting period of 21 days.
