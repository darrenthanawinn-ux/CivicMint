[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 5.02.010]
General Business License Requirement. Every person conducting, managing, or
carrying on any business within the City must obtain a general business
license from the Office of the City Clerk prior to commencing operations.
Application must include: legal business name, business address, owner
contact information, and a brief description of business activities.
Processing time is typically 5-10 business days. Base fee is $85, plus $12
per employee beyond the first 5 employees.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 5.02.030]
Renewal. General business licenses expire annually on December 31st and must
be renewed by January 31st of the following year to avoid a 15% late penalty.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 5.02.045]
Home-Based Business Exception. A home occupation permit (see Section 17.44)
substitutes for the general business license only where the business has no
employees on-site other than residents of the dwelling and generates no more
than 25% of total household floor area in commercial use.
