[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 17.44.020]
Home Occupation Permit. Residents operating a business from a residential
dwelling must obtain a Home Occupation Permit from the Planning Department.
No more than one non-resident employee may work on-site. Signage visible from
the public right-of-way is prohibited. Fee: $45, processed administratively
within 5 business days with no public hearing required.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 17.62.300]
Tenant Improvement / Building Permit. Any interior alteration affecting
plumbing, electrical, structural, or HVAC systems -- including standard
restaurant or retail buildout -- requires a Building Permit from the
Department of Building & Safety prior to work commencing. Plan check fee is
calculated at 0.65% of estimated construction valuation (minimum $150).
Typical plan check turnaround is 15-20 business days for commercial tenant
improvements.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 17.72.410]
Outdoor Seating / Sidewalk Cafe Permit. Placement of tables, chairs, or
merchandise displays on public sidewalk area requires an annual Sidewalk
Cafe Permit ($150/year) and proof of $1,000,000 general liability insurance
naming the City as additional insured. A minimum 5-foot unobstructed
pedestrian path must be maintained at all times.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 17.80.055]
Signage Permit. Any exterior sign larger than 6 square feet, or any
illuminated or projecting sign of any size, requires a Sign Permit from the
Planning Department ($95 flat fee) prior to installation.
