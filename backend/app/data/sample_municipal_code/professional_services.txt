[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 5.10.200]
Professional Services Registration. Businesses providing consulting, legal,
accounting, design, or similar professional services with no on-site
inventory or walk-in retail component may qualify for the streamlined
Professional Services Registration in lieu of a full commercial Certificate
of Occupancy review, provided the space was previously approved for office
use. Fee: $60, processed within 3 business days.

[SOURCE: Springfield Municipal Code | SECTION: 5.10.220]
State Professional Licensing Cross-Check. Certain professional service
categories (legal, accounting, cosmetology, contracting, real estate)
additionally require a valid State-issued professional license on file with
the City Clerk before the local registration will be issued.
