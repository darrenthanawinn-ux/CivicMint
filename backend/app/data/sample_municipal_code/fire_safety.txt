Fire Department Operational Permit. Businesses with an occupant load over 50,
or that use commercial cooking equipment producing grease-laden vapors
(restaurants, food trucks with cooking equipment), must obtain an annual Fire
Department Operational Permit. Requires inspection of fire extinguishers,
exit signage, and (for cooking operations) the Ansul/hood suppression system.
Fee: $175/year. Re-inspection after a failed initial inspection is $60.

[SOURCE: Springfield Fire Code | SECTION: FC-105.6.45]
Assembly Occupancy Permit. Bars, nightclubs, and any venue with a posted
occupant load over 100 persons intended for social gathering must obtain an
Assembly Occupancy Permit, renewed annually alongside the operational permit
inspection. Fee: $210/year.
